01 — A welcome that feels personal

Use a hand-written place card, a local flower, or a note that explains why everyone is here.

02 — Plan for everyone (Accessibility)

Keep a clear route for wheelchair users, a quiet corner, dietary labels, seating choices and accessible toilets in your venue checklist.

03 — Make it lighter on the planet

Choose seasonal or rented decor, reusable serveware, refill stations and a safe plan for leftover food.

04 — Give the day a story

Build the event around a shared memory, a favourite song, a community achievement or a family tradition.
